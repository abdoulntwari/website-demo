PROGRAMME

Twenty-one days, three chapters

Gates 10:00 – 22:00 daily. Stage programme every evening from 18:00.

WEEK 1 · 28 JUL – 3 AUG

Opening & Made in Rwanda

Ribbon cutting on 28 July, free entry until 14:00. Local manufacturers take Halls A and B through the first weekend.

WEEK 2 · 4 – 10 AUG

Agri-business & regional pavilions

Machinery demos in the outdoor yard, twenty country pavilions, and Taste of Africa across the food court.

WEEK 3 · 11 – 17 AUG

Youth, tech & closing concerts

Innovation Dome pitch nights, students half price, and the closing weekend line-up on the Main Arena stage.
